## Frozen population and boundaries

The original curriculum contains **160 statements in sixteen explicitly identified families**. It covers continuation/predicate logic, relation closure, partial orders, lattices, monoids, noncommutative groups, commutative rings, images, preimages, differences, inverse functions, curried applications, products, dependent contexts and heterogeneous transport. Each family has ten different statements; they are not renamed copies of a single equation. Forty statements in four whole families (continuation logic, lattice maps, set difference and product graphs) form selection validation. The other 120 form selection training. After hyperparameter selection, permitted development is refit; validation results are not independent final-test results.

The **48 final statements in twelve families** combine longer pipelines, noncommutative context systems, polynomial constraints, mixed lattice intervals, images/preimages, nested partitions, quantified case witnesses, dependent graphs, relation diamonds and inverse chains. These are generated before solver access; all unsolved tasks will remain. This is a benchmark of new compositions, not a claim of mathematical novelty or arbitrary logical independence. Shared proof primitives and mathematical structures are intentional.

Native reducible typed expression graphs are audited against all 212 earlier statements, within development, and between development and final evaluation. Graph isomorphism permits alpha renaming and forall-binder permutation. Six initially overlapping development statements were replaced **before** the freeze; no final task was removed in response to search. This does not decide arbitrary theorem equivalence; manually named families complement the check. The old datasets and models are hash-protected in the new freeze.

## Algorithms, allocation and order

New methods share unchanged search, independent complete-proof verification and axiom auditing. A 24-candidate cap applies everywhere. The context policy uses native snapshot heads and typeclass hypotheses to decide whether to request retrieval. Propositional goals without relevant mathematical structure retain symbolic generation. Existential goals reserve up to eight novelty slots; other retrieval contexts reserve four. The remaining expanded baseline lane protects goal/specialized actions before other actions in the old order. Available novel actions must pass native compatibility, have no unresolved explicit data, and avoid four generic projection/elimination proposals. Ineligibility is not mathematical invalidity.

The new expression closure promotes partial applications into callable terms. It records actual inferred types and parent derivations. It is bounded by depth three, 24 original locals, 64 terms, 24 functions per stage, 96 application probes and at most 128 total construction attempts including type-directed pairs; expression/type node caps are 64/96. Existing ground subterms from actual local hypothesis types are legitimate context evidence, not theorem answers. No theorem IDs or scripts enter generation. Unrestricted enumeration is avoided; arbitrary higher-order synthesis remains incomplete.

All new generators return canonical tactic-string order. The final scheduler is explicitly frozen P3 score followed by tactic-string tie order, so changing retrieval scores cannot silently reorder an identical selected pool. Historical arms preserve their original stable ties. A separately named Phase 4 canonical control measures this intervention. `naive` is the named baseline-first concatenation/truncation control; a saturated baseline may leave zero typed entries. `fixed` always reserves four novelty slots. `staged` uses symbolic candidates for the first two non-existential expansions before the deterministic context gate; existential roots retrieve immediately. There is one search/session/global budget; no restarted free search.

## Development evidence and learning

Three equal-budget searches per development task compare expanded symbolic, deterministic hybrid and the old deterministic typed generator. They share eight seconds, 160 attempts, 28 expansions, depth ten and 24 candidates. Source paths are chosen by actual independently verified completion and measured attempts. At most two actual states per task are probed, with sixteen actually executed alternatives and four bounded continuations each. Strong normalizers and generic compatible alternatives are explicitly sampled at the **same exact native state**. All replays, rejected applications, accepted unknown branches, censored continuations and independently audited completions are retained.

Candidate relevance compares verified source/continuation actions against actually rejected applications, and strictly different costs among two verified fixed-policy continuations. Resource errors, unexplored actions and censored searches are never mathematical-negative labels. An applicability-only control instead learns actual acceptance against actual rejection, with no downstream-usefulness claim. Actual goal reductions, remaining obligations and branching are retained as outcome diagnostics; successor features never enter pre-action inference.

A transparent full-batch pairwise linear model (L2 0.01 or 0.1) competes with independently implemented CPU histogram-CART gradient boosting (depth one/two, 24/48 trees, rate 0.1, minimum leaf 12). Candidate feature names contain no theorem identity or future outcomes. The boosted method fits pairwise-logistic pseudo-responses. Whole-family validation mean loss across fixed seeds 0/1/2 selects architecture and settings; final seed zero is fixed. Linear and applicability controls remain separate regardless of selection. Complexity is justified only if the preregistered validation rule selects it.

The adaptive selector predicts `log(1 + attempts)` **conditional on independently verified completion** for symbolic, hybrid and typed modes, using only pre-decision context and mode features. Linear ridge and depth-two boosted regressors compete by whole-family validation MSE. Censored modes remain in collection but are excluded as regression targets. This introduces success-conditioned selection bias and does not estimate provability or bounded-success probability. The learned policy chooses the minimum predicted conditional cost; it may choose an unsuccessful mode. This limitation and actual failure frequencies must accompany its results. Candidate usefulness and mode selection are distinct learned functions.

## Final comparison and measurements

Fourteen primary arms cover old expanded/P3, old typed/P3, old learned retrieval/P3, naive union, deterministic context hybrid, learned hybrid, linear learned hybrid, staged hybrid, fixed quota, old synthesis, applicability relevance, canonical old baseline and independent Aesop/grind. Ten arms repeat twice; four ablations once. Four preregistered policies also run once at a smaller budget. The total matrix is **1344 searches** on 48 statements. Training seeds measure model variation; repeated searches measure execution variation, not additional independent theorems.

Primary budgets are eighteen seconds, 600 attempts, eighty expansions, depth twelve, cap 24, two seconds per tactic/query. Small budgets are ten seconds, 240 attempts and forty expansions with the other caps unchanged. Every query and inference consumes the same outer wall time; queries are not free tactic attempts and their probes/heartbeats/costs are reported separately. Aesop/grind receive one external two-second call, not 600 comparable elementary inferences. `simp_all`, ring/omega and group `simp only` remain visible strong symbolic operations, never learned internal work.

Primary contrasts are learned versus strongest old baseline, deterministic versus strongest old baseline, and learned versus deterministic. Paired theorem averages receive 10,000-draw intervals with seed 2718. Equal-family sensitivity has its own estimand. Effort on common-verified tasks is reported separately from total resource consumption; failed cheap searches are not efficiency wins. Secondary contrasts and multiple families are descriptive, without hidden multiple-testing selection. Linux live-session CPU and sum of process RSS high-water bounds are partial practical metrics; independent audit CPU/RSS and simultaneous physical peaks are unavailable. End-to-end wall includes audits. Shared-host timings do not establish hardware speedups.

The model and exact execution source will be hash-frozen and committed before the first final test run. There will be no final-test retuning. Positive, negative and inconclusive outcomes will all be preserved.
