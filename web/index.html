                01 / FORMAL ENVIRONMENT
                
Lean is the authority.

                

                  A persistent Lean 4 elaborator executes every tactic and
                  returns structured goals and hypotheses. Each branch restores
                  a complete saved elaboration state.
                

              

              

                02 / ORIGINAL SEARCH
                
Search, step by step.

                

                  ProofLab implements breadth-first and heuristic best-first
                  search. Candidate steps come from mathematical structure,
                  local hypotheses, and a small inspectable Mathlib lemma index.
                

              

              

                03 / INDEPENDENT AUDIT
                
Check it again.

                

                  A fresh Lean invocation reconstructs the complete proof and
                  checks its transitive axiom dependencies. Admitted proofs and
                  unapproved axioms are rejected.
                

              

              

                04 / RESEARCH INTEGRITY
                
Show the limits.

                

                  Search is bounded. Failure means no proof was found within the
                  budget, never that the statement is false. The learned policy
                  is a small CPU-trained linear ranker. Its scores order
                  tactics; Lean establishes correctness.
                

              

            

            

              
Built for the next question.

              

                Phase 2 trains only on permitted development proofs and actual
                Lean rejections. A frozen evaluation across new theorem families
                measures whether learned candidate ordering helps. All
                experimental failures remain visible.
